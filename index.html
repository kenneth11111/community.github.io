Airtel Money – How to Donate

  1. Open Airtel Money on your phone.
  2. Select Send Money.
  3. Enter 0750858803.
  4. Enter the amount you wish to contribute.
  5. Verify the recipient details and confirm your transaction.

MTN MoMo – How to Donate

  1. Dial *165# or open MTN MoMo.
  2. Select Send Money.
  3. Choose Other Networks / Send to Other Network.
  4. Select Airtel Uganda.
  5. Enter 0750858803.
  6. Enter your donation amount.
  7. Verify the recipient details and confirm using your PIN.
Important: Always verify the recipient's name before confirming your transaction. If the recipient details do not match, do not proceed. MTN menu options may vary.
